# Privacy Notice

**Effective Date:** May 13, 2026

This Privacy Notice describes how JDevSolutions (“we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when customers install or use our Zendesk applications and related services (“Applications”).

By installing or using the Applications, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Notice.

---

# 1. Scope

This Privacy Notice applies to all Zendesk applications, integrations, and related services developed and distributed by JDevSolutions, including but not limited to:

- ConfigMap
- Conversation Analysis
- Future Zendesk marketplace or private applications

This Privacy Notice does not apply to third-party services, platforms, or applications not operated by JDevSolutions.

---

# 2. Information We Access

Depending on the functionality of the Application, we may access or process information available within a customer’s Zendesk instance, including:

- Ticket metadata
- Ticket comments and conversations
- Ticket fields and forms
- Triggers and automations
- Macros and views
- Groups and brands
- SLA policies
- Custom ticket statuses
- User and agent identifiers
- Tags and configuration objects
- Usage and diagnostic information

Applications only access information necessary to provide their intended functionality.

---

# 3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through the Applications may be used to:

- Provide application functionality
- Display or analyze Zendesk configuration relationships
- Search and analyze ticket conversations
- Generate reports, insights, or operational analysis
- Improve application stability and performance
- Troubleshoot technical issues
- Maintain security and prevent misuse

We do not sell customer data.

---

# 4. Data Storage and Retention

Depending on the specific Application:

- Some data may be processed temporarily in-memory only
- Some settings or preferences may be stored locally within the browser
- Limited operational or diagnostic logs may be retained for maintenance and troubleshooting purposes

Unless explicitly stated otherwise within the Application documentation or agreement, customer Zendesk content is not permanently stored outside of Zendesk systems beyond what is reasonably necessary for application operation, caching, security, or troubleshooting.

Retention periods may vary depending on operational requirements.
